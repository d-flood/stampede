Leave review comments on the lines of a pull request the user has open as a PR review in Stampede, instead of in the chat. `pr` names it. show: its Source commit, how to read its change, and its Draft comments. comment: a Draft comment on `path` at `line`, or `start_line` through `line`, with Markdown `body` and `commit` the Source commit show gave. `side` is `source` (default; lines as the pull request leaves them) or `target` (lines it removes or changes, numbered as they were). Lines must lie within one hunk of the diff, context lines included. A ```suggestion fence in `body` proposes replacement lines for the commented range. edit: replace the `body` of your `draft`. delete: delete your `draft`. Your comments stay Suggested until the user accepts them, and only the user sends the review to GitHub.
